# Admin Panel: Sales and Commission Management

The "Sales" and "Commissions" sections in the admin panel allow you to track all sales transactions and commissions paid to influencers on the platform in detail. These sections are critical for overseeing and reporting financial processes.

## 1. Sales Page

This page lists all sales made through influencer codes. For each sales record, you can view the following information:

*   **Date:** The date the sale occurred.
*   **Code:** The discount code used for the sale.
*   **Customer:** Information related to the customer (e.g., customer URL).
*   **Product:** The product or service sold.
*   **Amount:** The total amount paid by the customer.
*   **Commission:** The amount of commission earned by the influencer from this sale.
*   **Note:** Additional notes related to the sale (if any).
*   **Action:** Option to edit or view sales details.

### Filtering and Searching Sales

Various filtering and searching options are available to manage the sales list more easily:

*   **Code:** You can filter sales by a specific discount code.
*   **Date Range:** You can list sales that occurred within a specific start and end date.
*   **Influencer ID:** You can view sales belonging to a specific influencer.

### Editing Sales Details

You can edit the details of each sales record. This is particularly useful for incorrect amounts or missing information. Editable fields typically include amount, customer URL, product, and notes.

### Exporting Sales Data

You can export sales data in CSV or XLSX (Excel) format. This feature is useful for detailed reporting and analysis.

## 2. Commissions Page

This page lists all commission records, whether paid or to be paid, to influencers. It works in conjunction with the commission information on the sales page.

*   **Date:** The date the commission was earned or paid.
*   **Influencer:** The name of the influencer who earned the commission.
*   **Code:** The discount code through which the commission was earned.
*   **Sale Amount:** The total amount of the sale from which the commission was calculated.
*   **Commission Rate:** The commission rate the influencer earned from this sale.
*   **Commission Amount:** The net commission amount earned by the influencer.
*   **Status:** The current status of the commission (e.g., Paid, Pending).

### Filtering and Exporting Commissions

Similar to the sales page, you can filter the commission list by various criteria and export it in CSV or XLSX format.

The Sales and Commission Management sections allow you to transparently monitor and manage the financial flow of the AfiLite platform. This way, you can effectively oversee both your brand's revenues and your influencers' earnings.
