# Admin Panel: Payment Management

The "Payments" section in the admin panel allows you to manage and track all payment transactions made or to be made to influencers. This section is a central control point for ensuring financial processes are executed accurately and on time.

## 1. Payments Page

On this page, all registered payment requests and past payments in the system are presented in a list. For each payment record, you can view the following information:

*   **Date:** The date the payment process was initiated or completed.
*   **Influencer:** The name of the influencer to whom the payment will be made.
*   **IBAN:** The last few digits of the bank account number to which the payment will be sent.
*   **Amount:** The amount to be paid or already paid.
*   **Previous Balance:** The influencer's balance before this payment was made.
*   **Current Balance:** The influencer's balance after this payment was made.
*   **Status:** The current status of the payment (Pending, Completed, Canceled).
*   **Note:** Additional notes or explanations regarding the payment (if any).

### Filtering and Searching Payments

Various filtering and searching options are available to manage the payment list more easily:

*   **Influencer ID:** You can view payments belonging to a specific influencer.
*   **Date Range:** You can list payments that occurred within a specific start and end date.
*   **Status:** You can filter payments by their status (e.g., only "Pending" ones).

### Creating New Payments

As an admin, you can manually create new payment records for an influencer. This process is typically used for special situations or urgent payments that fall outside automated payment systems.

*   **Select Influencer:** Choose the influencer to whom the payment will be made.
*   **Amount:** Enter the amount to be paid.
*   **IBAN:** Enter the IBAN number to which the payment will be sent.
*   **Note:** Add a descriptive note about the payment.
*   **Status:** Set the initial status of the payment (e.g., Pending).

### Updating Payment Status

You can update the status of an existing payment record (e.g., from Pending to Completed). This is important for tracking payment processes and keeping records up-to-date.

### Exporting Payment Data

You can export payment data in CSV or XLSX (Excel) format. This feature is useful for financial reporting and accounting operations.

The Payment Management section allows you to keep your financial operations on the AfiLite platform under full control and transparently manage your payment relationships with your influencers.
