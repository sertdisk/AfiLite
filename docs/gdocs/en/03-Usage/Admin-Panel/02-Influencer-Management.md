# Admin Panel: Influencer Management

The "Influencers" section in the admin panel allows you to view, manage, and examine detailed information about all registered influencers in the system. This section is a central point for evaluating influencer applications, updating their profiles, and tracking their performance.

## 1. Influencer List

On this page, all registered influencers in the system are presented in a list. For each influencer, you can see the following basic information:

*   **Registration Date:** The date the influencer registered with the system.
*   **Influencer Code:** The discount codes belonging to the influencer (there can be multiple).
*   **Code Status:** Whether the code is active or not.
*   **Brand Name:** The brand name represented by the influencer (if any).
*   **Full Name:** The full name of the influencer.
*   **Email:** The influencer's contact email address.
*   **Balance:** The influencer's current balance (earned commissions - paid amounts).
*   **Action:** A link to go to the influencer's detail page.

### Filtering and Searching the List

Various filtering and searching options are available to manage the influencer list more easily:

*   **Search Bar:** You can search for influencers by their codes, brand names, full names, or email addresses.
*   **Date Filters:** You can specify a start and end date to list influencers registered within a certain date range.

### Sorting the List

You can sort the list by different criteria to access the information you need more quickly:

*   **Registration Date:** By the influencer's registration date (newest to oldest or oldest to newest).
*   **Brand Name:** Alphabetical sorting by brand name.
*   **Full Name:** Alphabetical sorting by the influencer's full name.
*   **Balance:** By the influencer's balance (highest to lowest or vice versa).

## 2. Influencer Detail Page

By clicking on the "Detail" link of an influencer from the influencer list, you can access all information related to that influencer. This page offers comprehensive tools to manage the influencer's profile and deeply analyze their performance.

### General Information

The influencer's basic contact information, status, and about information are located here. You can edit and save this information.

*   **Full Name, Email, Phone, Brand Name, About:** Influencer's profile information.
*   **Status:** The influencer's status in the system (Pending, Approved, Rejected, Suspended). You can update this status.
*   **Tax Type:** The influencer's tax liability type (Individual, Company).

### Balance and Performance Information

The influencer's financial status and performance metrics are summarized in this section:

*   **Current Balance:** The influencer's current balance.
*   **Total Commission:** The total commission earned by the influencer over all time.
*   **Paid Commission:** The total amount of payments made to the influencer.
*   **Unpaid Commission:** The influencer's unpaid commissions.
*   **Total Sales:** The total number of sales made with the influencer's codes.
*   **Last Payment Date:** The date of the last payment made to the influencer.
*   **Balance Movements:** A summary graph of total earnings, paid, and unpaid commissions.

### Platforms (Social Media Accounts)

You can view, edit, or delete the influencer's registered social media accounts.

### Payment Accounts

You can view and delete the bank account details where the influencer receives commission payments.

### Discount Codes

You can list all discount codes belonging to the influencer, create new codes, or update the rates of existing codes.

### Sales

You can list all sales made with the influencer's codes. You can view and edit the details of each sale (date, code, customer, product, amount, commission, note).

### Payments

You can list all payments made to the influencer, create new payment records, or update the status of existing payments.

The Influencer Management section is at the heart of the AfiLite platform and gives you full control to effectively manage your brand's relationships with its influencer network.
