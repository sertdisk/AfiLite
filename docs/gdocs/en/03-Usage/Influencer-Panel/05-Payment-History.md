# Influencer Panel: Payment History

This section provides a detailed record of all payments made to you through the AfiLite platform. You can track when and how much your earnings have been paid here.

## 1. Payment History List

On the payment history page, you will find a list containing the following information for each payment transaction made to you:

*   **Date:** The date the payment was made.
*   **IBAN:** The last few digits of your bank account number to which the payment was sent (full IBAN is not displayed for security reasons).
*   **Amount:** The net amount paid to you.
*   **Previous Balance:** Your total balance before this payment was made.
*   **Current Balance:** Your remaining balance after this payment was made.
*   **Status:** The current status of the payment (e.g., Completed, Pending, Canceled).
*   **Note:** Additional notes or explanations regarding the payment (if any).

## 2. Payment Statuses

Payment transactions can have different statuses:

*   **Completed:** The payment has been successfully transferred to your bank account.
*   **Pending:** The payment process has been initiated but is not yet complete. It usually completes within a short period, depending on bank processing times.
*   **Canceled:** The payment transaction has been canceled for various reasons. You can contact the admin for detailed information.

## 3. Payment Details

You can access more detailed information about a payment by clicking on each payment record or using the relevant action button. These details may also include information about which sales the payment originated from (if the system tracks at this level).

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions

*   **Why is my payment delayed?** Payments are usually made periodically (e.g., monthly). If the payment period has passed and your payment is still pending, you can check bank processing times or contact the admin.
*   **I entered the wrong IBAN, what should I do?** You can update your IBAN information from your profile settings and inform the admin about the situation. Refunds or re-sending of payments due to incorrect IBAN may take additional time.

Your payment history page is an important tool for keeping your financial records organized and transparently tracking your earnings.
