# Influencer Panel: Profile Settings

The profile settings page is a central area where you can manage your personal information, social media accounts, and payment details. Keeping your information current and accurate is critical for both your communication with brands and the smooth processing of your payments.

## 1. General Information

In this section, you can view and update your basic information such as your name, email address, phone number, and brand name.

*   **Full Name:** Your full name.
*   **Email:** Your email address used to log in to the system. (Usually cannot be changed).
*   **Phone:** Your phone number used for communication.
*   **Brand Name:** Your brand name, if you represent a brand or have your own brand.
*   **About:** A brief description introducing yourself and your niche. Helps brands understand you better.
*   **Website:** The address of your personal website or portfolio (if any).

After updating this information, you can save the changes by clicking the "Save" button.

## 2. Social Media Accounts

You can add, edit, or delete your social media platforms that brands will use as a reference when collaborating with you.

### Adding a New Social Media Account

*   **Platform:** Select which social media platform you are adding (e.g., Instagram, YouTube, TikTok).
*   **Username/Channel Name:** Your username or channel name on the respective platform.
*   **Profile Link:** The direct URL of your social media profile.
*   **Niche:** Your main content topic or area (e.g., Beauty, Technology, Gaming).
*   **Role:** Your role on the platform (e.g., Blogger, Vlogger, Gamer).
*   **Follower Count:** Your current follower count on the respective platform.
*   **Average Views:** Your average view count for video content (if any).

After entering the information, you can save the new account by clicking the "Add" button.

### Managing Existing Social Media Accounts

All your added social media accounts are displayed in a list. For each account:

*   **Edit:** Use to update account information.
*   **Delete:** Use to remove the account from the list.

## 3. Payment Accounts

You can manage your bank account details in this section to ensure your commission payments are processed smoothly. For security reasons, make sure your IBAN information is correct and up-to-date.

### Adding a New Payment Account

*   **Bank Name:** The name of your bank.
*   **Account Holder Name:** The name of the account holder as registered with the bank.
*   **IBAN:** Your International Bank Account Number.

When you add a new payment account, it automatically becomes active, and all your other payment accounts become inactive. This ensures you always have a single active payment account.

### Managing Existing Payment Accounts

All your added payment accounts are displayed in a list. For each account:

*   **Bank Name, IBAN, Account Holder Name:** Account details.
*   **Status:** Indicates whether the account is active.
*   **Delete:** Use to remove the account from the list.

## 4. Change Password

It is recommended to regularly change your password for the security of your account. In this section, you can set a new password by entering your current password.

*   **Current Password:** Your currently used password.
*   **New Password:** Your desired new password (must be at least 6 characters).
*   **Confirm New Password:** Re-enter your new password to confirm.

You can change your password by clicking the "Update Password" button.

Regularly checking and updating your profile settings will make your experience on the AfiLite platform smooth and secure.
