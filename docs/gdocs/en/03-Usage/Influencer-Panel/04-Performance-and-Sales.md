# Influencer Panel: Performance and Sales

This section allows you to track your sales performance and earnings on the AfiLite platform in detail. You can view records of every sale made through your codes, your commissions, and your overall performance trends here.

## 1. General Performance Summary

On your dashboard, you can find a quick summary of your overall performance:

*   **Current Balance:** Shows your current total earnings. This balance includes your unpaid commissions.
*   **Total Commission:** The total amount of commission you have earned through your codes over all time.
*   **Paid Commission:** The total amount of payments previously made to you.
*   **Unpaid Commission:** The total amount of earned commissions that have not yet been paid to you.
*   **Total Sales:** The total number of sales made through your codes.

## 2. Recent Sales

This section lists your most recent sales made with your codes. For each sales record, you can see the following details:

*   **Date:** The date the sale occurred.
*   **Customer URL:** The URL of the product or service page the customer was directed to (if available).
*   **Product:** The name of the product or service sold (if available).
*   **Amount:** The total amount paid by the customer.
*   **Commission:** The amount of commission you earned from this sale.

### Navigating Sales History

If you have many sales, you can navigate through your sales history using the pagination controls. This allows you to easily access all your sales records.

## 3. Performance Chart

The "Overall Performance Chart" section visually presents your sales count and commission trend over the last 30 days. Through this chart, you can:

*   **Sales Trend:** Observe the increase or decrease in your sales over a specific period.
*   **Commission Trend:** Track the change in your earned commissions over time.

This visualization helps you understand when you are most effective and optimize your marketing strategies accordingly.

## 4. Payment Information

This section contains information about your latest payment:

*   **Last Payment Date:** The date of your most recent payment.
*   **Payment Amount:** The amount transferred to you in the last payment.
*   **Balance Before Payment:** Your total balance before the last payment was made.
*   **Balance After Payment:** Your remaining balance after the last payment was made.

### Full Payment History

To review your complete payment history in detail, you can go to the relevant page using the "Payment History" link. This page provides a list of all payments made to you.

The Performance and Sales section provides you with comprehensive data to measure your success on the AfiLite platform and determine your future strategies.
