# Influencer Panel: Registration and Login

The AfiLite influencer panel is designed to help you easily manage your affiliate marketing process. This section provides a step-by-step guide on how to register and log in to the system.

## 1. Influencer Registration Form

If you don't have an AfiLite influencer account yet, you can access the registration form from the homepage. This form collects essential information for brands to contact you and track your performance.

*   **Access:** You can view the registration form by navigating to `http://localhost:4000/`.

### Registration Form Fields

*   **Full Name:** Your full name.
*   **Email:** Your email address, which you will use to log in to the system. All notifications will be sent to this address.
*   **Phone:** Your phone number for communication purposes.
*   **Social Media Links:** Links to your active social media platforms (Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, etc.). This field is important for brands to get to know you better.
*   **Follower Count:** Your total follower count across social media platforms.
*   **IBAN:** Your bank account number where commission payments will be made.
*   **Tax Type:** Your tax liability status, either individual or company.
*   **About:** A brief description introducing yourself and your niche.
*   **Message:** Any additional messages or notes you wish to convey to brands.

After filling out and submitting the form, your application will be automatically approved, and an influencer account will be created in the system. You can then proceed to log in.

## 2. Influencer Login

Once you have completed your registration, you can log in to your influencer panel. The login page provides access using your email address and password.

*   **Access:** You can view the login page by navigating to `http://localhost:4000/login`.

### Login Credentials

*   **Email:** The email address you used during registration.
*   **Password:** The password you set during registration.

Upon successful login, you will be redirected to the [Influencer Dashboard](./02-Dashboard.md) page. If you forget your password, you can initiate the password reset process using the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.
