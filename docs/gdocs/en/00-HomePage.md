# Welcome to the AfiLite Documentation

**AfiLite is a modern, discount code-based affiliate marketing platform that makes it easy for brands to increase sales while helping influencers earn revenue.**

Here's how it works:
- A **customer** gets a discount using a code.
- An **influencer** earns a commission from the same code.
- The **brand** increases its sales and visibility.

This win-win-win model ensures a reliable and transparent system.

## Why AfiLite?

AfiLite is not just a system for developers to install and use; it also offers a direct revenue model for influencers.

- **For Brands:** The most economical and measurable way to increase sales.
- **For Influencers:** A simple, secure, and prestigious method to earn income by sharing codes.
- **For Customers:** An opportunity to get real discounts on purchases.
