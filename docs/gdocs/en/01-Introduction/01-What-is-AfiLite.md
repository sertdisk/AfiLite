# What is AfiLite?

**AfiLite is a modern, discount code-based affiliate marketing platform that makes it easy for brands to increase sales while helping influencers earn revenue.**

In this system, everything is based on a simple and transparent win-win-win model:

- The **customer** gets an instant discount when they make a purchase using the influencer's code.
- The **influencer** earns a commission at a specified rate for every sale made through that code.
- The **brand** increases its sales and strengthens its brand awareness by reaching its target audience more organically.

## Core Purpose and Philosophy

The core philosophy of AfiLite is to eliminate the complexity in the world of affiliate marketing and create an ecosystem that is accessible, fair, and motivating for everyone. Unlike traditional advertising models, this performance-based structure means that brands only pay for actual sales. Influencers, in turn, receive tangible income in return for their content and the trust they have built with their audience.

## Why Should You Use AfiLite?

- **Flexible and Dual-Mode Architecture:** AfiLite is designed for both non-technical users and professional developers.
  - **Headful Solution:** With ready-to-use **Admin** and **Influencer** panels available right after installation, you can start managing the system immediately. No coding knowledge is required.
  - **Headless Solution:** Thanks to its powerful API infrastructure, you can easily integrate AfiLite with your existing websites, mobile applications, or any custom project. The user interface is entirely under your control.

- **Security and Transparency:** In a system where money and trust are central, security is the top priority.
  - **JWT (JSON Web Token):** All user sessions are protected with modern and secure JWT standards.
  - **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** The Admin and Influencer roles are clearly separated. Each user can only access data within their authorized scope.
  - **Transparent Reporting:** Influencers can track their sales, earned commissions, and payment history in real-time from their own dashboards.

- **Developer-Friendly:** Built with modern and lightweight technologies, AfiLite offers maximum freedom to developers.
  - **Lightweight Infrastructure:** Thanks to lightweight technologies like Node.js, Express, and SQLite, your server costs are reduced, and the system runs with high performance.
  - **Easy Installation:** You can get the system up and running in minutes with a few simple commands.
  - **Customizability:** Its open-source nature allows you to modify and enhance any part of the project to fit your specific needs.

- **Community-Focused:** AfiLite is not just a piece of software; it is a growing open-source movement. By starring, forking, or contributing code directly, you can become part of this ecosystem and help shape the future of the influencer economy.

This project is a powerful and flexible solution that caters to a wide audience, from small entrepreneurs selling digital products to large brands, and from content creators to developers.
